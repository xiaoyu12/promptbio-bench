# Treeness: animals vs fungi (single-copy orthologs)
- animals: n=100, median 0.000, mean 0.0091 (78% of genes have treeness 0)
- fungi: n=249, median 0.0501, mean 0.0525 (15% zeros; one outlier at 0.55)
- Two-sided Mann-Whitney U test: U = 3556 (animals as first sample; equivalently 21344 for fungi), p = 2.09e-26.
- Fungal single-copy ortholog gene trees have significantly higher treeness than animal ones.
