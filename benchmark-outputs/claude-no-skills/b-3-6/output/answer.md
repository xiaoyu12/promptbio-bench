# EGFR expression vs drug IC50 (Pearson, n = 80 cell lines)

Top 3 positive correlations (higher EGFR goes with higher IC50, i.e. more resistance): Afatinib r = 0.858, Gefitinib r = 0.857, Erlotinib r = 0.840.
Top 3 negative correlations (higher EGFR goes with lower IC50, i.e. more sensitivity): Cetuximab r = -0.888, Osimertinib r = -0.883, Lapatinib r = -0.830.
All six have p < 1e-20. The next strongest is Brigatinib (r = 0.32, FDR = 0.011). The other 13 drugs have |r| < 0.2.
