# Alzheimer's vs Control cognitive scores (n = 50 each)
- Means: Control 96.62 (SD 14.01), Alzheimer's 85.27 (SD 13.11)
- Mean difference (Control - AD): 11.35 points (Welch 95% CI 5.97 to 16.74)
- Welch's t-test: t = 4.18, df = 97.6, p = 6.3e-05
- Cohen's d (pooled SD) = 0.84, 95% bootstrap CI 0.46 to 1.24 (10,000 stratified resamples, seed 42, percentile method)
- Interpretation: **large** effect (|d| >= 0.8). Alzheimer's patients score significantly lower than controls. The CI's lower bound (0.46) sits at the small/medium boundary, so the true effect could be moderate.
