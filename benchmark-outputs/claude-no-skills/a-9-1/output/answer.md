# MHC-I neoantigen candidate peptides from chr20 somatic VCF

**Method:** I annotated the Mutect2 VCF (hg38, 124 records, which became 135 alleles after splitting multi-allelic sites) with varcode 1.1 and pyensembl against Ensembl GRCh38 release 112. I kept protein-altering effects on complete protein-coding transcripts: missense, in-frame indels, frameshifts and stop-loss. Transcripts had to be Ensembl canonical, MANE Select or TSL1. If a variant hit none of those, I used its other protein-coding transcripts instead. I then listed every 8-, 9-, 10- and 11-mer of the mutant protein that overlaps an altered residue (for frameshifts, the whole new sequence up to the new stop codon). I removed any peptide that also appears in the wild-type protein and removed duplicate sequences.

**Result:** `peptides.fa` has 777 unique peptides from 20 protein-altering alleles in 14 genes: TGM3, SMOX, ANKEF1, BANF2, CD93, NXT1, HCK, UQCC1, ERGIC3, TTI1, PLCG1, SLC9A8, SPO11 and FAM217B. By length there are 180 8-mers, 189 9-mers, 199 10-mers and 209 11-mers. Each FASTA header records the gene, transcript, protein change, variant, peptide start position and how many sources produced it.
