PRS computed with PLINK 1.9 `--score` (sum of BETA x effect-allele dosage over 1893 QC-passing SNPs; MAF>=0.01, geno<=0.1).
ROC of PRS vs binary phenotype (60 cases / 140 controls): **AUC = 0.7523** (bootstrap 95% CI 0.670-0.827). ROC curve: PRS_ROC_curve.png.
Caveats: sumstats P values are all >1 (invalid), so no p-value thresholding was applied; .bim alleles are A/B-coded, so the effect allele was taken as the .bim A1 allele.
