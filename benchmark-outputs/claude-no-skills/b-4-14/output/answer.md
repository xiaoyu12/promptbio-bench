# Spearman correlation: Shannon index vs CRP (n = 120)

- Spearman's rho = 0.4147
- Two-sided p-value = 2.49e-06
- 95% CI (Fisher z-transform) = 0.254 to 0.553

The correlation is moderate, positive and statistically significant (p < 0.001): patients with higher Shannon diversity tended to have higher CRP in this dataset. A bootstrap CI (0.254 to 0.561) and a Fieller-adjusted CI (0.244 to 0.560) agree closely with the Fisher interval.
