Reshaped data/bio_dataset.csv from wide to long format with pandas `melt`: sample = PatientID, gene = the 5 `*_expression` columns with the suffix removed (BRCA1, TP53, EGFR, MYC, KRAS), and expression = the raw value. The output, output/gene_expression_long.csv, has 2500 rows (500 samples x 5 genes) and the columns sample, gene, expression. The 156 missing expression values are left as empty cells, with no imputation.
