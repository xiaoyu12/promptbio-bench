Spatially variable genes were identified with Moran's I (squidpy.gr.spatial_autocorr, Visium hex-grid 6-neighbour graph, log-normalized expression, analytic p-values with BH correction) on 14,654 genes across 1,172 QC-passing spots. 4,773 genes have FDR < 0.05 (see spatially_variable_genes.csv); the strongest are AC108519.1, KRT10, KRT17, KRT1, KRT19, CST6, FOXCUT, KRT7, COL1A2, SAA1.
