# MR results (5 SNP instruments, MendelianRandomization 0.10.0, default random-effects)
- IVW: beta = 0.360 (SE 0.077, 95% CI 0.209-0.511), P = 2.96e-6 -> significant positive causal effect of expression on phenotype.
- MR-Egger: beta = 0.555 (SE 0.268, 95% CI 0.030-1.080), P = 0.038; intercept = -0.045 (SE 0.060, P = 0.45) -> no evidence of directional pleiotropy.
- Cochran's Q = 0.76 (P = 0.94): no heterogeneity. Both methods agree in direction, supporting a causal effect.
